## What it is

Omela is a coordination platform built around one everyday problem: chasing repeat prescriptions. I am building it on my own, from the product idea and positioning through to the code and deployment.

## How it is built

- **Frontend and application:** Next.js and TypeScript.
- **Data:** Prisma as the data layer over a Neon PostgreSQL database.
- **Sign-in:** NextAuth for authentication.
- **Hosting:** deployed on Vercel.

The site includes a landing page, a sign-in flow and a user portal. Where there is no data yet, the portal shows an honest empty state rather than invented demo records.

## What it shows

Taking a product from idea to a working full-stack application: data modelling, authentication, deployment, and writing clear product copy for non-technical users.

## Status

Live at omela-rho.vercel.app as an early-stage product. It is not a clinical service, and it is not integrated with any NHS or pharmacy system.
