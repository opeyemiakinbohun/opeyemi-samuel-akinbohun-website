I support first-year computing students in practical laboratories for CFS2101 Computer Organisation and Architecture, helping them connect theory to working C programs, digital logic and binary arithmetic.

## What the labs cover

- Introductory C programming: variables and data types, input and output with `scanf` and `printf`.
- Number systems and binary arithmetic.
- Boolean algebra, logic gates and Karnaugh maps.
- Adders, subtractors and structured problem-solving.

## How I teach

Most of the job is guided debugging. When a student's program fails, I help them read the error, form a hypothesis about the cause and test it, rather than fixing it for them. In a mixed-ability group that often means explaining the same idea in two or three different ways.

I am certified in Learning and Teaching in Higher Education, Part 1 (LTHE 1) at the University of Huddersfield, and LTHE 2 is in progress.
