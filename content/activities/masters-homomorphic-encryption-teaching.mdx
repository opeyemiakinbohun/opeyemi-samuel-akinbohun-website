I taught homomorphic encryption to master's students on the Applied Cryptography module at the University of Huddersfield, working alongside the module's academic staff.

## Lecture

I prepared lecture slides introducing homomorphic encryption and the Paillier cryptosystem, the scheme at the centre of my PhD research.

## Practical exercises

I designed hands-on Python exercises in which students encrypt values with Paillier, combine ciphertexts and decrypt the result, so they could see additive homomorphism working rather than only reading about it.

## Why it matters

Teaching the topic at master's level is a good test of understanding. It also connects directly to my research: the same ideas I implement in the testbed are what I explained to the class.
