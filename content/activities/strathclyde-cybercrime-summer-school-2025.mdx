I was selected for a SPRITE+-supported place at the University of Strathclyde Cybercrime Summer School in Glasgow, from 25 to 29 August 2025. SPRITE+ funded registration, travel and accommodation for its sponsored PhD participants.

## What the week covered

- A safe, contained phishing exercise using GoPhish and MailHog, run in a lab environment rather than against real targets.
- Deepfake detection and the security of retrieval-augmented generation systems.
- AI and the law, and individuals' data rights.
- Intrusion detection for smart factories, CHERI capability-based security and ransomware negotiation.
- Research networking with people working on cybercrime from technical, legal and social angles.

## What I took from it

The week widened how I think about cyber security, from technical, legal and social angles at once.

My reflection appears in a SPRITE+ article published on 16 October 2025 and updated on 5 November 2025.
