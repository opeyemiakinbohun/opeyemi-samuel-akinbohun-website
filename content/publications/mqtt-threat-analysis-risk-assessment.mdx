## The problem

Threat and risk assessments for IoT systems are usually built from expert judgement and risk matrices. They are rarely checked against how attacks behave in a working system. When the two disagree, it is not obvious which one is wrong.

## Approach

The paper develops a structured threat analysis and risk assessment for MQTT attacks in smart homes, then tests its assumptions against observations from controlled experiments on the testbed. It also examines how sensitive the resulting risk classifications are to changes in the assessment inputs.

## Why it matters beyond research

This is the kind of reasoning security consultants and risk analysts need: a risk rating that can be traced to evidence, with its assumptions stated and tested.
