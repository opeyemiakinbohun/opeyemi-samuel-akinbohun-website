## The problem

Smart-home devices produce a steady stream of readings about a household. Partially homomorphic encryption (PHE) lets a system compute on some of that data, typically sums, without decrypting it. Research on the idea is spread across cryptography, IoT systems and privacy engineering, and the studies rarely use the same assumptions, workloads or measures. That makes it hard to say what has been shown to work and what has not.

## What the review does

The paper is a systematic literature review. It follows a PRISMA-style search and screening process to identify studies that apply PHE to smart-home and closely related IoT settings, then organises them by technique, application, constraint and evaluation approach.

## What it contributes

- A structured view of the PHE techniques studied for smart-home workloads and how they have been applied.
- The performance, deployment and security challenges that recur across the literature.
- Future research directions, which informed the experimental work in the rest of my PhD.

## Connection to my research

This review is the foundation of my doctoral programme. The gaps it identifies shaped the testbed and the experimental papers that followed.
