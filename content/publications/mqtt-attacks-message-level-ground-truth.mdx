## The problem

Many IoT security datasets label traffic in time windows or by flow. That is often too coarse to say exactly which message was malicious, whether the broker accepted it and whether the receiving application acted on it. Without that, it is difficult to evaluate detection methods or defences fairly.

## Approach

The work captures controlled MQTT attacks in the smart-home testbed at more than one point in the message path and records ground truth for each message. Combining several observation points shows the difference between a packet that was transmitted and one that was received, processed and acted upon.

## Connection to my research

The capture and labelling methods developed here underpin the evidence in my other experimental papers, and the same approach to correlating captures and logs appears in my digital forensics work.
