## The problem

Smart-home automation often depends on knowing whether a room is occupied. Sensor messages can go missing through faults, congestion or deliberate suppression. A system that keeps making confident decisions on incomplete data can behave in ways users would not expect or accept.

## Approach

The study investigates occupancy prediction when sensor messages are omitted, with a focus on keeping decisions interpretable and on behaving cautiously when the evidence is incomplete.

## Connection to my research

Message omission is one of the conditions I study in the testbed alongside active attacks. This paper looks at its effect on the decisions a smart home makes, not only on the network.
